Parent decision: **Ti64 1877.15/1933.15 K as the named Gan fixed-front convention with a disclosed integer273 conversion interpretation; AlSi10Mg 840.15/872.15 K as the explicitly transferred Brandt heating-cycle convention.** Neither is the measured or assessed liquidus of the user's specimen. ### Contradictory Ti64 sources remain separate

**Cezairliyan et al. 1977**: PDF1/printed251 footnote explicitly defines this work's “melting point” as the alloy **solidus**. PDF3/printed253 gives the average initial-melting plateau of four tube specimens as **1942.6 K**, with average absolute deviation 0.4 K and maximum absolute deviation 0.8 K; Table 3 lists 1942.7, 1943.5, 1942.0 and 1942.4 K. PDF1 and PDF4/Table5 give an estimated total inaccuracy **8 K**, including random and systematic contributions; no confidence level is specified. It is not a 95% bound and not the quoted sample spread. Tube/rod measurements use resistive pulse heating with pyrometry, heating **2600-3800 K/s**, current pulses **400-500 ms**, argon at atmospheric pressure (PDF2/printed252). The temperature scale is IPTS-68; no unverified conversion to ITS-90 is applied. The reported rod state is hot swaged, primary alpha plus acicular alpha and retained beta (PDF1). PDF5/printed255 says collapse interrupted melting before completion: **there is no measured liquidus to pair with this solidus**.

Source rod chemistry (Table 1, PDF2): Ti 89.2, Al 6.1, V 4.2 wt%; remaining entries in ppm: B10, Ca50, Cr15, Cu30, Fe1500, Mg50, Mn30, Mo150, Ni15, Nb not stated (dash), Pb50, Si300, Sn300, Ta100, W50, Zr30, C220, H80, N120, O1700. The plate is a different lot and must not supply rod chemistry or the true-temperature solidus. Missing Nb is unknown, never zero.

The source measured solidus exceeds the interpreted convention liquidus1933.15 K by **9.45 K** (and remains1.45 K higher even after subtracting the source's 8 K estimated inaccuracy). This is a contradiction to treating the two as universal physical endpoints, not proof that either paper is wrong. Batch, temperature-scale, observable and rapid-heating differences have not been quantitatively reconciled. **Never pair Cezairliyan's solidus with Gan's liquidus, average the sources, or use their spread as prediction uncertainty.** This precludes an exact-physical-endpoint claim; it does not erase Gan's explicitly identified source-model convention.

**Boivineau et al. 2006**, DOI [10.1007/s10765-005-0001-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10765-005-0001-6): PDF3/printed509 quotes supplier Goodfellow's **1873-1923 K** melting range. This is a supplier declaration, not their measured endpoint pair. PDF16/printed522 derives **1928 K** from extrapolated emissivity at a transition **assumed** to be liquidus; original radiance temperature is **1788 K**. Its source explicitly notes the pure-metal-like transition and liquidus assignment assumption. It does not supply a separately measured same-cycle solidus temperature. PDF6 reports pulse-device heating-rate range 10^6-10^8 K/s; this range does not authenticate supplier endpoints. PDF4/Table I gives typical/standard chemistry, not a complete measured batch assay. Both supplier range and inferred liquidus remain separately named alternatives; neither closes the Cezairliyan/Gan contradiction or qualifies as an exact batch pair. The 2006 library's misleading old DOI header must not replace the DOI printed on article page507.

## AlSi10Mg admission record (RT1, RT6, M7)

- Source: Brandt and Neuer 2007, DOI [10.1007/s10765-006-0144-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10765-006-0144-0), PDF14/printed1442 Table II, sample **AlSi-10**, which means **9 mass% Si, 0.6 mass% Mg**, balance Al; it is not an assay of Al-10Si-Mg. Other elements/impurities are unreported, not zero. Source processing provenance: MEBSP alloys produced by Hydro Aluminium Deutschland, rod/furnace experiment, not an LPBF powder specimen (PDF3). Full prior heat treatment and trace chemistry are unknown.
- Original heating-cycle pair **567/599 C**, exactly **840.15/872.15 K**, interval32 K. Both endpoints belong to the same sample column within the source's labelled **Heating cycle** section. An individual run/cycle number or shared raw trace ID is not published: `same-reported-heating-cycle-pair=true`, `same-individual-trace=not-established`. Do not mix in a cooling endpoint.
- Method: four-probe electrical resistivity in a ceramic tubular crucible with three crucible thermocouples, high vacuum (PDF3,5,7-8). Reported Ts/Tl are changes identified on resistivity-versus-temperature heating curves. A specific onset/peak/end/return-to-baseline construction is **not defined**; do not invent a DSC onset/end method. The endpoint definition is `source-labelled-resistivity-transition-boundary; construction-unknown`.
- Rates: 360 K/h outside the phase-change region, interrupted before measuring points; reduced to **about100 K/h = about0.0277777777777778 K/s** during melting/solidification (PDF7-8). This is a prescribed apparatus rate, not an independently measured instantaneous specimen rate. Crucible/specimen temperature lag, and heating/cooling differences, are explicitly discussed (PDF5-6). An LPBF-rate equivalence claim is refused.
- Admission: named `Brandt2007-Al9Si0.6Mg-heating-transfer` as a nominal **AlSi10Mg fixed-front approximation**. Transfer changes **both Si9 to nominal10 mass% and Mg0.6 to the user's specified Mg**; do not silently assume a target Mg value from the family name. For comparison only, manufacturer MP07's product range is Si9-11 and Mg0.20-0.45 wt%, so the Brandt Mg is outside that particular product range. Source-to-current-specimen chemistry/state match is `not-established`, and arbitrary changed chemistry is not automatically admitted.
- Uncertainty: endpoint measurement and composition/state/rate transfer uncertainty **not-quantified**; electrical-resistivity or thermal-conductivity percentage uncertainties cannot be reused as endpoint K uncertainties. No fitted correction from9Si to10Si is applied.
- Parent identity: both source original-C values and exact K conversion, raw PDF hash/page/table/sample/heating-cycle ID, disclosed source/target chemistry and state, family-transfer convention, complete thermal field/process/settings identity, and all existing eligibility restrictions.
- Movement relative to served557/595 C (830.15/868.15 K): **Ts+10 K, Tl+4 K, interval38 to32 K**. This affects all liquidus-dependent means, contours, enthalpy terms and P8 source arms; the integration owner must enumerate/recompute those from the actual closure. It does not retroactively authenticate the old pair.

### Al alternatives and transfer sensitivity

The **same AlSi-10 column's cooling** pair is **549/596 C = 822.15/869.15 K**, interval47 K. It is an alternative cycle convention, not a lower/upper uncertainty bound; heating/cooling differences are -18 K for Ts and -3 K for Tl. No hybrid heating-solidus/cooling-liquidus pair is admitted.

Table II heating liquidus values for `(Si,Mg)=(5,0.6),(7,0.6),(9,0.6),(12,0.3),(12,0.6)` mass% are respectively **634,621,599,609,590 C**. At Mg0.6 the adjacent Si-only secants are **-6.5, -11, -3 K per mass%-point Si**, not one universal3-7 range; including the `(12,0.3)` point confounds Mg and Si. The gate's approximate3-7 K/wt% lead is therefore not adopted as a transfer error bar. These composition-dependent differences make the9Si-to10Si transfer material; they do not establish a linear correction or an uncertainty interval at10Si.

The [3D Systems Rev D declaration](https://www.3dsystems.com/sites/default/files/2023-12/3d-systems-laserform-alsi10mga-mds-letter-us-revd-web.pdf), PDF2 “Thermal Properties”/footnote16, prints a typical **557-596 C** range (**830.15/869.15 K**). It explicitly classifies the value as literature-based, with no primary method/cycle/rate/endpoint definitions or source-assay relationship. It is retained as a product declaration alternative, not promoted to measurement and not chosen merely because it is near the served557/595 C.

## Reproduction limits

The source-bound JSON checks should fail if an endpoint table cell, cycle label, source chemistry, method rate or raw PDF bytes changes; testing a deliberately modified expected endpoint must fail. These checks authenticate transcription/pairing and arithmetic only. They do not estimate systematic endpoint errors, validate a composition transfer or resolve the missing upstream ASM table. The admission exception for a source-model convention must remain explicit for every alloy, including Gan SS316 and secondary IN625 model prescriptions.
