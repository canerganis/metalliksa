## Independence and exclusions

No source read here proves the actual endpoint lineage of the shared Sabau/SMC/Mills-Kim/JMatPro numbers. This remains explicitly unresolved. Consequently a thermal-versus-CALPHAD signed discrepancy cannot be described as independent validation for IN625. A source-model admission is possible while physical provenance and independence remain unresolved because those are distinct claims. Cieslak family DTA alternatives must retain their individual chemistry, method and paired temperatures; none is averaged into this convention.

`docs/research/sol_t_in625_checks.json` specifies raw hashes, page/crop locators and extraction regexes. The integrated `python -I ... --check` checker reads archived source bytes and independently extracts the actual paired print, while failure on a wrong hash, wrong capture, changed pair or source-origin text is observable. In particular it does not merely assert one hand-entered ledger against itself. The Sabau crop is baseline coordinates in PDF points, bottom-left origin: `60<x<320`, `225<y<300`, PDF7 height792. Only the property lines reach the checker; neither the page's measured-track paragraph nor its numerical values are emitted. The Gan2019 property crop is PDF7 `45<x<300`, `640<y<669`; ref44 crop is PDF17 `300<x<594`, `410<y<430`. NFKC is required to normalize the source's ligatures. This is source transcription verification, not scientific validation.
