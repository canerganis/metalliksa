## Why the old identity is insufficient

The existing SPEC section 4.1 defines a fixed-liquidus level set and section 9 Gate 3 requires an independent travelling-field derivative. The re-gate dated 2026-10-15, D/RC2, shows that checking only `G R = v gx` misses errors in `gy`, `gz` and `G`: with `R = v gx/G` those terms cancel. A componentwise oracle must precede that identity.

For a fixed threshold `F = T - TL`, differentiating `F(xI(t),t)=0` gives `partial_t F + VI dot grad F = 0`. The normal toward hotter material is `n = grad F / |grad F|`, so the signed interface speed is `R = -partial_t F/|grad F|`. For `Tlab(x,y,z,t) = Tbeam(x-vt,y,z)` it is `R = v gx/G`; solidification requires `gx > 0` for positive scan speed. The positive local cooling magnitude is `-partial_t Tlab = v gx = G R`. For the explicit nonsteady case `Tlab = Tbeam(x-vt,y,z)+b t`, use `R = (v gx-b)/G`; the steady projection alone is then wrong. These are calculus derivations, not fitted or externally sourced constants, and do not identify interface speed with fluid velocity.

   `T = 300 + 1800 exp[-(x/160um)^2-(y/90um)^2-(z/65um)^2]`.
   The independent reference derivative is `gi = -2 qi (T-300)/Li^2`. Test at `(-80,25,30) um`, where no component vanishes. With `h = 4,2,1 um`, each observed convergence order must be within `[1.95,2.05]`, and the last relative component error below `3e-4`. The manufactured dimensional inputs are arbitrary regression parameters, not alloy properties or measured temperatures. They make incorrect y/z gradients visible despite the physical sampler's `y=0` symmetry.
2. **Surface derivative.** At `z=0`, use `T=500+2e6 x+3e6 y+4e6 z+2e10 z^2` with all lengths in metres. For `h=8,4,2 um`, the exact relative z-derivative truncation error is `2e10 h/4e6`. Successive errors must fall by a factor within `[1.95,2.05]`. This is the first-order one-sided stencil actually in production; it detects the historical `2h` denominator that halved the derivative. No second-order surface accuracy is claimed.
3. **Actual sampler G, n, R and G/R.** Execute actual `_binary_z_liquidus` and `map_solidification_front` with the anisotropic field, a manufactured threshold 1200, scan speed `0.8 m/s`, rear search `133.2 um`, depth `100 um`, `h=0.2 um` and nine requested samples. At every recovered sample separately compare all three gradient components, Euclidean norm `G`, `nx`, `R`, cooling and `G/R` against the analytic field. Every relative error must be below `5e-5`; the manufactured liquidus residual must be below `0.1 K`. Require all nine captured points and emitted tuples to pair without loss.
4. **Actual served field, independent differentiation.** Extract `rosenthal_temperature_C` from `python/lpbf_thermal_solver.py` without importing that module. At three off-axis rear points, compare the actual derivative helper with the independent closed-form derivative of the actual regularized expression, away from its origin and exponent clipping. With `r=|q|`, `s=r^2+rreg^2`, `a=v/(2 alpha)` and `DeltaT=P exp[-a(r+x)]/(2 pi k sqrt(s))`, the reference is `gi = DeltaT[-qi/s-a(qi/r+delta_ix)]`. Steps `0.8,0.4,0.2 um` must give component orders within `[1.9,2.1]` and last relative errors below `2e-4`. Again all scalar field inputs are manufactured, with no alloy assignment, empirical calibration or endpoint claim.
5. **Laboratory versus beam frame and static fluid.** Independently difference `Tlab=Tbeam(x-vt,y,z)+b t` in time with `dt=1 ns`, `b=2e5 K/s`. Compare its derivative and the derived normal speed to the analytic formula above within relative `1e-7`. Require a moving interface with fluid velocity zero and failure of the steady projection for this nonsteady fixture. This checks the proposed mathematical contract; the production sampler remains steady-only and must refuse a nonsteady field until a suitable time derivative is available.
6. **Live-source mutation sensitivity.** Mutate extracted AST copies in memory only: force `gy=0`, halve `gz`, double `G`, replace `R=v nx` by `R=v`, and reverse `gx`. Every mutant must fail the component or actual-sampler acceptance. The control must pass all clauses. The gy mutant specifically uses the nonzero-y fixture; it cannot pass vacuously because the current sampler samples a symmetry plane.

## Tolerances and runtime uncertainty are different

The fixed-fixture tolerances are numerical verification budgets, not confidence intervals. For the Gaussian central stencil the exact derivative-to-reference ratio is `exp[-h^2/Li^2] sinh(2 qi h/Li^2)/(2 qi h/Li^2)`, with its continuous limit at zero. Its Taylor expansion gives second-order error; the one-sided polynomial gives the first-order surface error exactly. This explains the observed orders and why a universal relative `3e-6` without a stencil rationale was unsuitable. The analytic budgets here admit the specified stencil errors with margin and detect the stated mutations. They must not be copied into user-facing physical uncertainty.

For each future runtime front tuple, retain component derivatives, actual stencil kind and step, threshold residual, root bracket width and evaluations at `h` and `h/2`. An interior Richardson indicator is `|D(h/2)-D(h)|/3` under demonstrated second-order convergence; for a first-order one-sided derivative it is `|D(h/2)-D(h)|`. At the production `80 nm` step floor, two equal clamped steps provide no convergence evidence: report an unassessed/refused numerical bound unless another independently checked derivative is available. Finite-difference numerical error, root-location error, spread across the sampled front and uncertain physical inputs are separate contributions. Recompute the co-located `G,n,R,cooling,G/R` for each admitted input realization and threshold; do not propagate independent aggregate G/R medians or assume their errors are independent.

## Checked result and remaining integration gates

The 2026-10-16 run passed: nine actual sampler points; maximum relative errors `G=6.427e-6`, `R=5.234e-6`, `cooling=1.556e-6`, `G/R=1.167e-5`; largest manufactured liquidus residual `0.031713 K`. Central component orders `1.99826..1.99993`; actual Rosenthal component orders `1.99969..2.00057`; all five mutations rejected. The transient normal speed was `0.2880077393 m/s` while fluid velocity was zero.

This closes the demonstrated **RC2 cancellation hole** for the checked helper/sampler and regularized Rosenthal implementation. It does not make the served result non-null or qualify material predictions. RC1 request/channel/serialization is unimplemented here. The future integration must replay the same independent component/norm/projection tests against the exact runtime producer and transported unrounded tuples, including source/root/stencil identities. Eagar-Tsai and Goldak kernel-specific derivative-reference checks, exponent clipping/nonsmooth Rosenthal branches, arbitrary runtime support/convergence checks, spatial quadrature coverage, and physical input/model uncertainty remain separate gates. The generic derivative helper tests alone do not establish those kernel references or justify non-null values for an unsupported kernel. No morphology, PDAS, SDAS or phase admission follows from a kinematics pass.
