**What P6 is.** A containment bump: every served number that the G1 audit classed as an uncited heuristic or a clipped artefact in the surface, recoil, Marangoni, denudation and interface block is either replaced by a sourced quantity with a stated validity condition or served as `null` with an unavailable reason. No new physics closure is added (the P6 onset re-solve is NOT implemented, sensitivity only; P3 is untouched). Melt-pool width, depth, length, contours, process-map grid, Tang, balling, regime labels and the Build Job verdict inputs do not change. P6 makes no accuracy claim; `experimentalValidation=false`, `validationStatus=unvalidated`, `productionReady=false`, N3 NOT PASSED, N4 NOT EVALUATED, no evidence label or user-facing wording is promoted.

## 1. Served change (the ten gate items, with locators)

Line numbers refer to `python/lpbf_thermal_solver.py` (TS), `python/solidification_front.py` (SF), `python/eagar_tsai_solver.py` (ET) at base `65a34457`.

1. **peakTemperature_C** (TS 713, 1125; B01). The regularised Rosenthal centre value `T0 + P/(2 pi k r_reg)` is removed as a served peak for every kernel. Served instead: the loss-free Eagar and Tsai 1983 Gaussian surface maximum (`eagar1983.pdf`, printed p.347-s Eq. 6, 8 to 10, p.348-s Eq. 11, Appendix B), evaluated as the numerical maximum along the axis y = z = 0 of the served Eagar-Tsai field (`eagar-tsai-v2`, ET 71 to 138) driven by the same field power `A P`, blend `k`, `alpha` and 1/e^2 radius as the requested kernel, with its x location. New function `eagar_tsai_surface_maximum` in ET (own model identity, key `peakTemperatureModelId = "eagar-tsai-v2"`, `peakLocation_x_um`). It is served only when it is below a SOURCED vapour temperature (item 2); otherwise `null` with the reason that a centreline above boiling is an impossible situation for the loss-free solution (Eagar and Tsai 1983 p.348-s, Results of the Model). The peak depends on the kernel only through the shared inputs, so every kernel serves the same value; `peakTemperatureBasis = "eagar-tsai-loss-free-surface-maximum"`.
2. **Sourced T_v per alloy** (new table `SOURCED_VAPOR_TEMPERATURE_K` in TS): Ti-6Al-4V 3560 K (Gan et al. 2021, Nat. Commun. 12:2379, Supplementary Table 1, `gan2021_SI.pdf` p.20, "Vaporization point T_v (K)" Ti64 column, verified on the PDF text); 316L 3086 K (Khairallah et al. 2016, Acta Mater. 108, Sec. 2.2, printed p.38, "T_b = 3086 K is the boiling temperature of 316L stainless steel", verified on the PDF text). Inconel 718, AlSi10Mg and every other alloy: `null` (no sourced vapour data in the library; no elemental proxy; lead decision). Alias resolution through `four_alloy_materials.resolve_alloy_id`. The unsourced served `boiling_C`, `latent_heat_vap_J_kg` and `M_molar_kg_mol` of the material authority (`four_alloy_materials.py` 81 to 90, 106 to 115, 131 to 140, 156 to 165; `SECONDARY_THERMOPHYSICAL_DB`) stay untouched: they still drive the Fabbro leg (`T_vap` at TS 700, G1 B04) and are flagged in a comment and in the bump reasons as out of P6 scope. The P6 served T_v never enters the leg.
3. **axialFieldMaximum_C** (TS 717, 1091): same policy as the peak. For Eagar-Tsai and Goldak it is the kernel's own axial maximum at `peakOffset_um`, served only below the sourced T_v, else `null` with reason. For Rosenthal it is the regularised singular-source value and is served `null` with that reason (interpretation I-1, below). **peakExceedsVaporization** (TS 1128): the loss-free maximum compared with the sourced T_v; `null` where T_v is unsourced. **surfaceTemperature_C** (TS 763, 1130): equals the served peak, else `null`; the clip `min(peak, T_vap)` at TS 763 is deleted and no served temperature is ever clipped.
4. **knudsenRecoilPressure_kPa** (TS 307 to 319, 779, 1131; B03): `null` in every regime; `knight_recoil_pressure_kPa` and the constant 54.72 kPa (0.54 atm at T_v) are removed. Served instead `recoilOnsetStatus`: "below Fabbro 2020 App A onset" when the loss-free maximum is below the sourced T_v, "above onset, no wall closure" when it is at or above it, `null` with reason where T_v is unsourced (interpretation I-2: Fabbro 2020 App. A Eq. A10 to A12 give the onset `p_r(T_th) = p_amb` at `T_th = T_v / (1 - ln(2 p_amb / ((1 + beta) p0)) / c)`, 3.5 to 4.4 % above T_v for the Gan source alloys; the comparison is made against the sourced T_v, which is a lower bound of T_th, and a loss-free maximum at or above T_v is not a wall temperature, so neither recoil nor the onset margin is resolvable without a wall closure; `recoilOnsetBasis` says so).
5. **marangoniNumber**, `surfaceVelocity_m_s`, `pecletMarangoni` (TS 781 to 791, 1118 to 1120, 1132; `marangoni_screening.py` 64, B07): the temperature difference is the served peak minus liquidus when the peak is served, else these three are `null` with reason (`marangoniUnavailableReason`); `flowDirection`, `dGamma_dT_N_mK` and `sulfur_ppm` do not depend on temperature and stay (new helper `marangoni_flow_direction` in `marangoni_screening.py`; `marangoni_screening` itself is unchanged so the G12 parity observations of it do not move). **powderDenudationWidth_um** (TS 836 to 837, 1136; B05): `null` with reason, the heuristic `W (1 + 0.35 min(3, p/30))` is removed.
6. **G, R, cooling rate** (SF 139 to 286; B13, B14, B15): the field-mapped rear-liquidus path is kept and labelled `gradientSource = "conduction-field-interface"` (interface: rear liquidus isotherm of the loss-free conduction field in the y = 0 plane, Ghosh et al. 2017 introduction, P6 derivation section 7 Eq. 13). Served `null` with reason when the served depth comes from the Fabbro keyhole leg (`e_kh > d_iso`, TS 733: the conduction field does not describe the keyhole interface). The tail-length fallback (SF 234 to 244, `G = max(10, T_surface - T_sol) / x_rear`) is deleted; fewer than 3 solidifying samples gives `null` with reason. The incline multiplier `max(0.05, cos theta)` (SF 148 default, 156, 179; TS 596, 850, 1166) is removed: `R = v n_x`; `solidificationCosTheta` is no longer served. The floors `G >= 100`, `R >= 1e-4`, `Tdot >= 1` (SF 241 to 248) and `g_over_r >= 1` (SF 251) are removed. The one-sided surface derivative (SF 48) no longer divides a one-sided difference by `2h`: when `z - h < 0` the z derivative is `(T(z + h) - T(z)) / h`. PDAS, SDAS, morphology, `g_over_r_ratio`, `coolingRate_log10` and the phase-transformation note propagate `null`. `evaluate_solidification` keeps accepting its former keywords (`t_surface`, `x_front`, `cos_theta`, `pdas_A1`, ignored) so the guarded parity harness is not edited.
7. **thermalSlices and colour scale**: the Python `thermalSlices` carry no cap (unchanged). The TypeScript colour-scale top (`src/utils/peakTemperatureDisplay.ts`) is a display cap `max(sourced T_v or 3500 C fallback, 1.5 liquidus)`, used when the served peak is `null` or above it, and is labelled "display cap, not a temperature" wherever it is printed (`MeltPool3DCrossSectionLab.tsx` 1150, `meltPool3DGeometry.ts` 83).

Build Job verdict (`python/lpbf_build_job_solver.py` `compose_verdict` 324 to 652): a `null` gate input (Tang index, W, D, dH/hs, distortion index) marks that gate `not-evaluated` with a reason, never pass or fail; a not-evaluated verdict-driving gate makes the verdict `inconclusive` and the headline says so; peak, recoil, G/R and microstructure are not verdict inputs and an `assumptions` line says so.
