## Scope and decision

Restoring a co-located liquidus-front tuple does not supply nucleation density, nucleation undercooling, substrate grain orientation, remelting history, multicomponent interfacial stability or subsolidus time-temperature history. Therefore SOL-C admits local thermal metrics, subject to its own gates, but admits **no default LPBF morphology/CET classifier or final phase prediction for any of the five alloys** on the evidence reviewed here. This is an evidence-bounded search result, not a claim that such models cannot exist.

The existing envelope supports `front-morphology` and `solid-state-transformation` as categories with **no admitted category members** (`python/quantity_envelope.py`, `QUANTITY_DEFINITIONS`); neither may contain a non-null value. `cet-class` allows `columnar`, `mixed`, `equiaxed`, but category spelling alone grants no physical admission. Do not reuse a CET class as a cellular/dendritic stability class or as a final phase identity. Do not turn a grain criterion's threshold fraction into an observed spatial grain fraction.

## Existing IN718 CET sources: retained documentary relation, no relaxed LPBF admission

The source-scoped Knapp and Polonsky relations, coefficients, dimensional discrepancy and printed-limit checks remain controlled by SPEC section 5.3. SOL-C does **not** promote their source-map arithmetic to the served LPBF grain class by attaching a screening label or a wide band. A literature-source label and module ceiling do not cure an absent process/field/parameter domain. No new constants are transcribed in this note.

An opt-in **source-model demonstration**, if a later independent contract requests one, would need positive separately paired local G and R, the complete bound coefficient set, source process and field identity, the existing process/field/numerical masks, explicit nucleation assumption and source-alternative uncertainty. Aggregate local classifications with the same declared spatial support/weights; never classify at separate G and R medians. A categorical uncertainty statement would list all admitted alternatives and classes reached, with no probability claim; crossing a class boundary means an unresolved class, not an averaged “mixed” class. This note supplies no new demonstration channel and admits no user-specimen CET output.

Consistency-note ordering is deterministic: (1) stale/missing parent, invalid sample or incompatible keyhole field; (2) alloy endpoint convention/transfer warning; (3) unavailable downstream model/history; (4) the bounded literature context. Never display “columnar expected” solely from high G/R, “equiaxed expected” solely from cooling rate, or a phase-presence probability unsupported by a calibrated model.
