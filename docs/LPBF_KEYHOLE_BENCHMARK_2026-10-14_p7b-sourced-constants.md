App screening evaluated: Screening classifier, not the papers' regime definition. Inputs: normalised enthalpy dH/h_s = eta*P / (rho*cp_s*max(50, T_liq-T0)*sqrt(pi*alpha_s*v*r^3)) (same form as lpbf_thermal_solver.py, flat-plate absorptivity_IR of the material authority, solid k/cp, r = d/2) and the dataset's own balling flag. balling == 1 -> 'balling-flagged' (Hofmann only); otherwise dH/h_s < 15 -> 'conduction', 15 <= dH/h_s < 20 -> 'transition', >= 20 -> 'keyhole'; Inconel 625: dH/h_s < 13.6 -> 'conduction', >= 13.6 -> 'keyhole', no transition band (derivation: lpbf_thermal_solver.REGIME_THRESHOLD_BASIS). Measured D/W is recorded next to the label but not used for it.

Regime threshold basis (solver): dH/hs < 15 conduction, 15-20 transition, >= 20 keyhole mode (melt-pool D/W > 0.5 screening onset); Inconel 625 uses its own cut: < 13.6 conduction, >= 13.6 keyhole mode, no transition band (see the IN625 alloy note). 20 is a provisional screening choice, not a derived exact threshold: it lies in the overlap of King et al. 2014 30+/-4 (316L) converted to the repo convention (19.3-25.2; conditional on the app absorptivity, 20 C preheat and property set; King's printed density unit kg/m3 corrected to 7980 kg/m3) and the Cunningham et al. 2019 Fig. 3A melt-pool transition line (Ti-6Al-4V; app index 17.3-20.0 along speed, a range, not a confidence interval). Both sources and the held-out checks are for Ti-6Al-4V and 316L; transferred, not derived, for IN718/AlSi10Mg and the other alloys. Cunningham and Gan data are not independent validation. Keyhole mode is not keyhole porosity; a low index is not an assurance.

## Threshold derivation (from code)

King et al. 2014 Table 3 (316L, Table 3 (King et al. 2014 accepted MS, OSTI 1502044); sigma: Section 3.2.1 fn. 3-4; 30 +/- 4: Section 5): H_King / H_app = **1.351** at equal P, v and beam (r = 2 sigma). King 30 +/- 4 corresponds to app index 22.213 (19.251 to 25.175). Cunningham 2019 Fig. 3A red line (Ti-6Al-4V 95 um): app index 17.293 to 20.005 along speed (a range, not a confidence interval). Overlap 19.251 to 20.005; chosen threshold 20 (inside both: True); a provisional screening choice. King's Table 3 prints density as '7.98 kg/m3' (verified on the rendered page image of the accepted manuscript, OSTI 1502044); the physical value is 7.98 g/cm3 = 7980 kg/m3, so the unit was corrected here, not taken as printed. Convention conversion at equal P, v and beam (r = 2 sigma), conditional on the app absorptivity, 20 C preheat and the app 316L property set; the app absorptivity enters the index linearly, so the converted threshold is consistent with the app's estimated absorptivity, not with the true one. The Cunningham red-line range is the app index along speed (400-1200 mm/s), not a confidence interval; the chosen 20 is a provisional screening choice, not a derived exact threshold.
